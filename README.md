# Ultimate Ascent

Adds seventeen new insanely hard ascents to **PEAK**, up to 25: new brutal hazards, harsher weather, worse items and much more. Originally inspired by [**13 Downright Dastardly Ascents**](https://thunderstore.io/c/peak/p/pooblives/13_Downright_Dastardly_Ascents/) and [**More Ascents**](https://thunderstore.io/c/peak/p/asdfmovie402/MoreAscents/), but also adds lots of our own original challenges.
The mod also adds new items, new creatures, some QoL additions and bug fixes. (Everything can be turned off in the config)

## Ascents list

* 9 Ascent - fog moves twice as fast, starts almost immediately.
* 10 Ascent - big luggage now have a chance to only contain one item.
* 11 Ascent - rule zero is stricter: Scoutmaster spawns more aggressively - if  you are 150m ahead of everyone and have no other scout 35m around you, he will spawn. You also gain curse when another scout dies, and eating scout poisons you. 
* 12 Ascent - falling or pulling someone by hand makes you drowsy.
* 13 Ascent - taking fall damage drops every item you hold, including your backpack.
* 14 Ascent - ailments apply more: cold and drowsiness x1.3, heat x1.5, spores x1.65, poison x1.75
* 15 Ascent - items are worse. Less food restored, less uses, less range. Everything is much worse.
* 16 Ascent - heat heals significantly slower in the Caldera and Kiln, drowsiness heals significantly slower in the Gloom and Citadel.
* 17 Ascent - hazards are more dangerous: thorns damages more, bees come at you in tropics, explosions are more powerful, campfires overcook your food and so much more.
* 18 Ascent - weather is more extreme: wind carries debris, rain is much more slippery, snowstorms and tornadoes are stronger, sun in mesa burns your items to ashes, lava in caldera rises higher, new hot events in kiln, gloom's fog applies more drowsy, nadir has higher gravity.
* 19 Ascent - no more revive statues.
* 20 Ascent - bing bong calls for your attention. You better hold him, or get cursed!
* 21 Ascent - all suitcases inflict damage upon opening. (Clown suitcases do something more chaotic and dangerous instead)
* 22 Ascent - ailments have unique effects: bleed from injuries, higher gravity from weight, drowsiness not heals at night, cold slows you down and if you have too much, you might pass out shivering for some time, heat makes you dizzy, spores periodically numb you, eating while poisoned may bring nausea.
* 23 Ascent - the sun is hot everywhere, except Alpine. Theres no daylight cycle in the caldera and gloom, so no hot sun there as well.
* 24 Ascent - going unconscious is instant death
* 25 Ascent - all weather everywhere, at slightly less effect. Rain, wind, sandstorm, tornado. Everything.

Also, there are now two additional challenge options to complete, with new cosmetic items in reward!
* Hot mode - the sun is hot! Always on from A23 onward.
* Blind mode - you cant see anything.

## New creatures

* Crabs on the Beach - crawl around from spot to spot and chase any scout within 2 m. Be careful with their claws!
* Snakes in Tropics - some sleep on the ground, some on top of the giant tree branches. Hisses when someone comes close, then chase and jump on you. You can throw rocks at them, or fight them with sticks. But their bite is poisonous, so dont get caught! 
* Mountain goats in Alpine - They are mostly peaceful, but if you stay close for too long, they might charge at you and knock you down! 

## New items
* Stones - Spawns on the ground. You can throw them at zombies or snakes to knock them down. 
* Sticks - Also spawns on the ground. You can hold it and defend yourself from zombies, snakes or other scouts by swinging it.
* Climbing Chalk - Spawns in chests, when used, climbing costs only 60% of stamina for 20 seconds. Has 3 uses.
* Auto-Parachute - Spawns in chests, uses the backpack slot, saves you from long falls. Has 8 charges.
* Vanilla Wafers - Spawns in chests, almost the same as scout cookies, but replenishes less hunger and gives more additional stamina.
* Cherry Lollipop - Spawns in chests, gives both Lollipop's infinite stamina and Energy Drink's energy boosts, but only lasts 6 seconds. Gives more drowsy.
* Warp Compass - Relic, spawns in statues or cursed chests. Can be used once to teleport to other random scout.
* Toxic Drink - Relic, spawns in statues or cursed chests. When drank, boosts your run and climb speed **four** times for 6 seconds. The buff is insane! But then it poisons you.
* Six-slot Backpack - only spawns in singleplayer, instead of the normal backpack. Has 6 slots. (you can turn it off, or even turn it on for multiplayer if you really want to)

Adds a few new cosmetic items - one for blind and one for hot mode challenges, one for completing 23 difficulty, and one, the most difficult one, for completing 25 difficulty. I dare you try earning it! I never did.

## QoL additions
* Adds ability to choose any map in the Airport kiosk.
* Now counts your attempts and wins on each ascent.
* You can now choose any ascent in custom runs.
* Alpine looks a little better now. It has falling snow!
* Fixes ***most*** of the vanilla bugs and game abuses (no more throwing yourself around the whole map with cannon or cactus).

## Fully compatible with:
* [True Final Ascent](https://thunderstore.io/c/peak/p/CptPICHU/TrueFinalAscent/) - you can use its new mode with new ascents, you can also play it with Apocalypse Mode if you're masochist.
* [Item Stats](https://thunderstore.io/c/peak/p/boxofbiscuits97/ItemStats/) - show nerfed item stats when on high enough ascent. Also fixes lag it makes by checking everything every frame.
* [Effect Preview](https://thunderstore.io/c/peak/p/OnlyCook/Effect_Preview/) - show nerfed item effect preview when on high enough ascent.
