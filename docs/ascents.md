# The ascents

[Ultimate Ascent](../README.md) documentation.

Ascents are cumulative, like the vanilla ones: ascent *N* contains every vanilla ascent and every rule of ascents 9
to *N*. Each row is that ascent's own rule; "13DDA" is 13 Downright Dastardly Ascents. Every threat of ascent 24 with
its numbers, against the game's, is in [the threats at ascent 24](threats-ascent-24.md); the new items and creatures
the rules mention are in [items](items.md) and [creatures](creatures.md).
