# Fixes to the game

[Ultimate Ascent](../README.md) documentation.

On every ascent (and below 9), the mod fixes these bugs and exploits of the game's own:

* **Warps in multiplayer (PEAK 3.1):** the game now lets only the host warp a scout, but a scout's own warps (the
  **Warp Fungus**, the **Warp Compass**) are sent from their own game, so for everyone but the host they did nothing.
  They now go through the host, who makes them (only of that player's own scout).
* **Warp Fungus:** warped onto a jumping mushroom mid-fall you bounce as from a standstill (not as after the whole
  fall); a warp goes only where there is room for you (no more into the rock or out of the map at a corner) and
  fizzles if there is none; an explosion throws it as hard as a scout (not many times harder); no warp, and no
  Teleportation Compass jump, goes through the Nadir's black wall while it stands.
* **Dropped items** (from the hands, any slot, the backpack, the backpack dropped to carry someone) land only where
  nothing solid lies between you and there, never inside the rock.
* **Jetpack:** no push while you are knocked down (an explosion or a Rescue Claw no longer shoots you sky high); it
  pushes as at 60 fps at any frame rate.
* **Fog walls** between the segments cannot be climbed or stood on.
* **Lava** gives no stamina (a lava pillar's had 10 for a scout it bounced off); it stays climbable.
* **Dynamite** explodes once. **Balloons** pop on lava and in an explosion that reaches them.
* A cooked **Anti-Zooka**'s bubble over another lifts you once, not enormously high.
* Carried by a **condor**, you cannot grab a rope or a vine (and let go of one you hold).
* A **checkpoint flag** brings you back with its statuses even if you died invincible (milk, a Shroomberry, a gem).
* Stuck on a **cactus**, stamina comes back only as it would unstuck (no endless climbing by breaking free).
* Going limp on a **jumping mushroom**'s bounce (a Chain Launcher's chain run out, playing dead) gives the plain limp
  bounce, not the long fall's, and a scout rising from a bounce keeps their air drag however late they go limp.
* A **backpack fired from the Scout Cannon** flies off without the scout in the tube.
* The **Scoutmaster** pulls a scout off a piton, a rope or a vine (he shook them forever); his throw never breaks
  off; he no longer turns round and round under a scout above him.
* **Ropes:** a rope stays when the host leaves; a scout who leaves no longer takes their ropes, anchors and Chain
  Launcher chains away from everyone (they stay, a rope hanging as it last was; players who join later do not see
  them); the airport's training rope comes back after a run.
* A scout left tied to the scout who carried them (both knocked out at once) is set free.
