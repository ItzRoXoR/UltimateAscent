# Challenges and cosmetics

[Ultimate Ascent](../README.md) documentation.

Two boxes in the boarding pass's header, **Blind Mode** and **Hot Mode**, work on every ascent. In multiplayer the
host's boxes count for everyone: the other players' passes show the host's ticks, dimmed, and cannot change them (their
own ticks are kept for when they host):

* **Blind Mode:** all climb long your screen stays as dark as the Scoutmaster makes it at his closest
  (without his sound). When he really comes, it gets no darker.
* **Hot Mode:** ascent 23's sun on any ascent below 23, where that sun would scorch (not in the Alps, not
  from the Caldera and the Gloom on): it incinerates whatever you hold up against it just the same, and heats
  you by about 0.33 a second. On ascent 23 and higher the box shows ticked and fixed: that ascent already has it.

The mod's cosmetics are in the passport, each unlocked by a challenge (clicking one while it is locked tells how):

* **VR Headset:** reach the peak with Blind Mode on for the whole climb.
* **Orange Spacesuit** and **Orange Helmet:** reach the peak wearing the Astronaut suit and the Astronaut
  helmet together for the whole climb, with Hot Mode on (or on ascent 23 or higher).
* **Pain Sash** (after the game's sashes): reach the peak on ascent 25 with all modifiers on for the whole climb (no
  MODIFIERS OFF line under the ascent at the top right). A dark sash soaked in blood, its stains slowly
  drifting. Whoever wears it leaves bloody boot prints at every step on the ground (not while climbing or
  in water), seen by every player with the mod; each print fades away within 10 s.
* **Burnt Skin** (after the game's skins): reach the peak on ascent 23 or higher with all modifiers on for the whole
  climb. A charred maroon skin in the Pain Sash's colours, the arms, legs and body spotted with char and small glowing
  embers; the face stays clean.

The first three are cosmetics the game made but never offered. Custom runs do not count. The run's
statistics show a new cosmetic as a small icon where the badges go. These cosmetics have no Scout badge and leave
the badge ribbon alone. What you unlocked, which of them you wear and your two boxes are kept in the mod's own save
next to the game's (`UltimateAscent/Save.txt`), not in Steam; players without the mod see the game's default outfit
and hat in their place, the game's last sash for the Pain Sash and the game's last skin for the Burnt Skin.
