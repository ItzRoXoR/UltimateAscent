# Multiplayer

[Ultimate Ascent](../README.md) documentation.

Everybody needs **the same version** of the mod. The boarding pass refuses to start ascents 9–25 (a custom run
on one of them too) and names who is missing it, in a line over the pass at its top left. The ascent itself is the game's own value, so it reaches every player the
vanilla way (also late joiners and campfire saves). The host's ascent switches and its Hot Campfires and Cold
Nights switches (see [configuration](configuration.md)) apply to everyone. During a climb, a line under the ascent at the top right names
the switches that ascent takes in that are off (the host's): **MODIFIERS OFF: 14, COLD NIGHTS**; with all of them on
there is no line. From ascent 5, in custom runs only with an ascent picked. The host's Blind Mode and Hot Mode count
for everyone too (see [challenges and cosmetics](challenges-and-cosmetics.md)).
